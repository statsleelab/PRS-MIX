LD block independence on chr22 — results

Companion to inst/scripts/compare_block_independence.R. PRS-CS treats SNPs in different LD blocks as uncorrelated, so a block layout is only as good as the LD it does not cut through. This note measures that for two chr22 panel builds that differ only in where the blocks are cut: the original fixed 250 kb tiles, and the block boundaries of PRS-CS's published 1000 Genomes EUR panel applied to every population.

Result. The fixed 250 kb blocks discard 32.0% of nearby LD in EUR and 27.7% in ASN. The PRS-CS EUR cut points discard 1.7% and 0.9%.

1. What was compared

Two chr22 builds of the EUR and ASN panels from generate_prscs_ld_panels() (33KG genotypes, corrected CalWgtCov() estimator, MAF ≥ 0.01 in both populations, HapMap3 SNPs only). Both contain the same 14,237 SNPs with identical positions, alleles, allele frequencies and order: all 19 checks in compare_panel_snp_sets.R passed. Only the cut points come from PRS-CS; every LD value is estimated from 33KG.

2. How independence is measured

For population $p$, take every pair of panel SNPs $(i,j)$ within 1 Mb of each other (6,329,000 pairs on chr22) and compute their LD $r_{ij}$ from 33KG genotypes. Sample $r^2$ is biased upward by roughly $1/n$, so each one is corrected before summing: $$ \tilde r_{ij}^{\,2} = r_{ij}^2 - \frac{1 - r_{ij}^2}{n_p - 2}, $$ with $n_p$ the population's sample size ($n_{\mathrm{EUR}} = 20,281$, $n_{\mathrm{ASN}} = 10,800$). Writing $b(i)$ for the block that SNP $i$ falls in, the share of nearby LD a layout discards is $$ \text{LD lost} = \frac{\sum_{i,j:\; b(i) \ne b(j)} \tilde r_{ij}^{\,2}}{\sum_{i,j} \tilde r_{ij}^{\,2}}. $$ PRS-CS sets every term in the numerator to zero, so this is the fraction of local LD it ignores under that layout.

4. Where the boundaries fall

Each line is one block boundary at its position on chr22; its height is the largest $r^2$ between any two SNPs it separates (within 1 Mb). Hovering a line shows its values.

The fixed tiles cut every 250 kb regardless of LD, and LD regions often extend over hundreds of kilobases, so most cuts land inside one. The published boundaries sit where LD drops off.

Limits: only pairs within 1 Mb are counted, which covers nearly all LD between HapMap3 SNPs but not rare long-range LD. This is chr22 only, and EUR and ASN only; in the earlier chr22 audit on all HapMap3 SNPs, AFR lost 11.5% with the EUR cut points (44% with fixed 250 kb), and no published block set did well for AFR. This measures how well each layout satisfies PRS-CS's block-independence assumption, not its effect on posterior SNP effects; that comparison runs next, on shared simulated data.
